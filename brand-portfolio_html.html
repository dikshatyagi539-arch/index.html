About me

Diksha Tyagi

Brand strategist in training. I find what a brand already has that's working, then build the plan that says so.

Postgraduate qualification in marketing and strategy from Warwick Business School

Hi, I'm Diksha. This portfolio is what I do when nobody's asked me to. I picked four UK brands and tried to work out what each one is really missing.

It's usually not more content. Gymshark proved community beats ad spend, and now every rival is running the same playbook. Charlotte Tilbury's products top the UK's most-searched-for beauty dupe lists, and there's no single campaign that owns that yet. Dyson still wins on engineering, but low-cost rivals have closed the gap it used to sell on. Once you spot that, the fix is often sitting in what the brand already has, so I build on that instead of starting from scratch.

I also like ideas you can actually run: a week of posts, a campaign with a hero piece and a way for customers to join in, not a slide that just says "be more authentic".

I don't know yet which team I'll end up on. If you're a brand with good stuff that can't quite say it in one sentence, we'd probably get along.

Method & frameworks

What's actually behind each chapter, so this reads as process rather than opinion.

  • Brand audit built as a strengths-vs-gaps read against each brand's own recent activity, not a generic SWOT template.
  • Competitor benchmarking against 2–3 named rivals per brand, using publicly reported positioning, scale and reputation data.
  • Content planning translated into a runnable 7-day calendar, format by format, not just themes.
  • Campaign ideas built to extend something the brand already owns, with a hero piece and a way for customers to join in.
